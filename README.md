# PC Optimizer (tester builds)

Early test builds of PC Optimizer, a game booster for Windows 10 and 11.

## Download

**[Download the latest build](https://github.com/Sup-yo/pc-optimizer-releases/releases/latest)**: on that page, under **Assets**, click `PCOptimizer-Setup-x.y.z.exe`.

## Install (once)

1. Run the file you downloaded.
2. Windows will say **"Windows protected your PC"**, because the app isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. If your antivirus blocks or removes it, tell me which antivirus. Don't turn your antivirus off to get around it.

You only install once. After that the app updates itself: it shows a notification, and **Settings > Install update** does the rest. Your settings are kept.

## What to try

- **PC Health**: does anything it says look wrong for your PC?
- **Boost** before a game. Pick what to keep open first. Does anything close that shouldn't, or refuse to open while the boost is on? Does everything come back after?
- **Benchmarks**: load into a game, then run one. It measures a normal minute and a boosted minute.
- **Tweaks** and **Debloat**: switch a few on. Everything can be switched back.

## Reporting a problem

Use **Report an issue** at the bottom of the app's sidebar. Say what you were doing, click **Create report**, then **Open in email**.

## Undoing everything

**Tweaks > Restore Original Settings** puts back every setting the app ever changed. Uninstalling does the same.

This is test software. Expect rough edges.
